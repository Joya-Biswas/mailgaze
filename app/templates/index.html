Headers are the hidden routing information attached to every email — not the message you read on screen. Your email client hides them by default. To get them:

  • Gmail: open the email → click the ⋮ (three dots) at the top right of the message → Show original → Copy to clipboard
  • Outlook: open the email → File → Properties → Internet headers
  • Apple Mail: View → Message → All Headers
  • Thunderbird: Ctrl+U

What you paste should start with lines like these: 

Delivered-To: you@gmail.com
Received: from mail.example.com (mail.example.com [203.0.113.5])
        by mx.google.com with ESMTPS id abc123
Authentication-Results: mx.google.com; spf=pass; dkim=pass
From: Someone <someone@example.com>
Subject: The subject line

What should it look like?

Headers are the routing information behind an email, not the message you read. Yours will be longer, but it starts like this: 

Delivered-To: you@gmail.com
Received: from mail.example.com (mail.example.com [203.0.113.5])
        by mx.google.com with ESMTPS id abc123
        for <you@gmail.com>; Fri, 7 Aug 2026 14:27:00 -0700
Authentication-Results: mx.google.com; spf=pass; dkim=pass; dmarc=pass
From: Someone <someone@example.com>
Subject: The subject line

Lots of Word: lines — that's the sign you have the right thing. Pasting the readable message body instead won't work.
